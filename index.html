The thinking behind it

Straight talk before you start

15 kg in 76 days is the wrong target: not because you're incapable, but because it fights the running. It needs a ~1,500 kcal daily deficit while you build to 40 km weeks. You'd bleed muscle, your long runs would fall apart around week 6, and the injury risk on a heavier frame running further goes up sharply.

So here's the deal I'd make with you as your coach:

Primary target: 9-11 kg down by 15 Nov. That's 0.8-1.0 kg/week, aggressive but survivable alongside this mileage. The last 4-6 kg lands over Dec-Jan, which is already your original 75 kg timeline, so you lose nothing.
Primary target: finish 21.1 km on Sun 15 Nov. Finish it, don't race it. Somewhere near 2:45-3:00 at conversational effort is a strong outcome from this base.

Your actual race is 14 Feb 2027 with a 2:30 goal. This block is the base and the first build. You'll have 13 more weeks after it to sharpen. Treating 15 Nov as a peak race would cost you February.

The one rule that makes Saturday work: everything below the waist on Saturday stops 3 reps short of failure. Saturday's lower session exists to keep tissue strong, not to build a squat. Shoulders take the hard volume; legs take the light volume. If Sunday's long run feels heavy two weeks running, cut Saturday's lower work in half.
Mon-Fri are built to a 90-minute door-to-door clock: ~12 min travel each way, 5 min warm-up, 50 min of work. Everything is supersetted (A1/A2 back-to-back). If you're short on time, cut the last block, never the first.
Non-negotiables
01Weigh daily, judge weekly.Same time, post-toilet, pre-food. Only the 7-day average counts. Daily numbers swing 1.5 kg on sodium and glycogen alone.
0210,000+ steps outside training.Your gym time is capped at 90 min. Steps are the only fat-loss lever you can expand without touching the schedule.
037 hours of sleep, floor not ceiling.Under-sleeping in a deficit shifts weight loss from fat toward muscle and blunts running recovery. This is a training variable.
04Easy days actually easy.You should be able to hold a conversation. If you can't, slow down or walk. Most people ruin a build here.
05Run before 6:30 am or after 7 pm.Mumbai September-October is brutal on humidity. Midday running will wreck your pacing and your fuelling.
06Sharp pain stops the session.Especially shins and knees. One missed session costs nothing. Six weeks off costs the block.
When you miss a day

You will miss sessions. Travel, work, an interview that runs long, a bad night. The block doesn't fail there, it fails in how you re-enter. These are the rules, decided now while you're calm rather than at 11 pm on a Wednesday.

01Never stack a missed session onto the next day.Two hard days back to back is how a missed run becomes a missed week. The session is gone. Let it go.
02The long run is the only one worth rescheduling.Move it to Saturday or Monday and shift that day's lift. Everything else you simply skip.
03Missed a whole week? Repeat it, don't skip ahead.Restart at the mileage you last actually ran, not the number on the plan. You lose one week, not six to an injury.
04Above the neck, train easy. Below the neck, rest.Blocked nose, mild sore throat: easy running is fine. Chest, fever, body aches, stomach: nothing until 24 h clear.
05A blown diet day ends at midnight, not at the weekend.One day over target costs roughly a day's progress. Abandoning the week costs the week. Log it honestly and carry on.
06If you're choosing, keep the long run and the protein.In a compressed week those two hold the block together. Everything else is optional in a way they are not.
What happens on 16 November

This block ends, February doesn't. From 16 Nov you get one properly easy week: no lifting below the waist, running only if you feel like it. Then 13 weeks of build into 14 Feb, starting from a 19 km long run rather than an 8 km one. The last 4-6 kg comes off across December and January at a gentler rate, because by then the training is sharper and the deficit has to get smaller. Nothing here is meant to be held at this intensity past 15 Nov.

Effort, not pace

You don't have a reliable pace history yet, so everything is prescribed by effort. Pace will follow as weight comes off. Expect roughly 20-30 sec/km faster per 3 kg lost, for free.

How to spend it
01Protein at every meal, 35-45 g each.Eggs, paneer, curd, dal + soya, chicken, fish, whey. In a deficit this is what decides whether you lose fat or lose muscle.
02Do not run low-carb.Rice and roti are training equipment here. Put the biggest carb meals around Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday sessions.
03Never start a long run fasted past 12 km.60-90 min before: a banana and toast, or poha, or 2 dates and black coffee. Test it in training, never on race day.
04Fuel during anything over 75 minutes.30-45 g carbs per hour: a gel, or dates, or diluted juice. Start this from week 5's 14 km run, so your gut is trained by November.
05Salt, not just water.Mumbai humidity means heavy sweat loss. 500-700 ml per hour of running with electrolytes. Plain water alone on a 2-hour run will leave you flat and cramping.
06Two free meals a week, planned.Put them Saturday and one weekday. A deficit you can hold for 11 weeks beats a perfect one you abandon in three.
07Alcohol is the first thing to go.It suppresses fat oxidation, wrecks sleep quality, and its calories buy you nothing at all in this block.
One caveat worth saying plainly

I'm not a dietitian and I don't know your bloodwork, medications or history. A deficit this size next to 40 km weeks is a real physiological load. If you have any metabolic, thyroid, cardiac or joint history, get this cleared by a doctor and ideally have a sports dietitian sanity-check the numbers against your actual body.
